# Licensing Plan

Original PuzzleForge code, documentation, and original vector artwork are distributed under MIT. Each dependency and bundled asset must retain its own copyright and license notices. Release review compares `pubspec.lock`, Android dependency output, and the asset manifest against `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

Generated puzzles are facts/mechanics produced by original algorithms; authored campaign content must be original or contributed under compatible terms. User-imported images or levels remain the user's responsibility and are not uploaded by the initial app.

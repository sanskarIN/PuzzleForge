# Buy Me a Coffee Support Experience

The in-app support card is an original PuzzleForge design: a simple coffee cup, heart-shaped steam, and forge spark. It does not copy Buy Me a Coffee's logo artwork.

The card opens `https://buymeacoffee.com/sanskarIN` only after deliberate activation and has the accessible label “Support Sanskar on Buy Me a Coffee.” It is available from Home, Settings, Support, About, documentation, and credits, but never interrupts active gameplay.

Donations do not unlock core mechanics, remove accessibility barriers, buy outcomes, guarantee features, or grant support priority. Clicks are not tracked. If external launching fails, the UI shows a localized message and leaves the URL available for copying.

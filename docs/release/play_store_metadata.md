## Full description

Think deeper. Solve smarter with PuzzleForge, an open-source, offline-first collection of logic and board puzzles built for focused play.

Explore eight original presentations of familiar puzzle mechanics: Sliding Tiles, Number Merge, Light Grid, Maze, Sudoku, Memory Match, Color Sort, and Number Sequence. Every enabled generator uses deterministic seeds and validates its opening before play. Daily content remains stable for the date and game version, even without a network connection.

Play your way:

- Choose Beginner through Master difficulty.
- Continue a saved puzzle or revisit recent and completed games.
- Explore Daily Puzzle, campaign, endless, and custom-seed modes.
- Use undo, redo, pause, restart, replay, and legal-step hints.
- Earn local XP, levels, stars, achievements, streaks, and personal records.
- Favorite puzzle types and compare local statistics.

Designed for access:

- English and Hindi interface choices.
- High-contrast, reduced-motion, numeric-label, spacing, and dyslexia-friendly preferences.
- Large touch targets and screen-reader descriptions.
- Patterns, labels, symbols, and numbers supplement color.
- Responsive layouts for phones, tablets, portrait, and landscape.

Private by default:

- No account is required.
- No advertising or analytics SDK is included.
- No remote gameplay service is used.
- Settings, saves, achievements, and statistics are stored locally.
- Backup export/import is user controlled and integrity checked.

PuzzleForge is open source under the MIT License. Contributions, security reporting, privacy details, and third-party notices are available in the public repository.

# PuzzleForge Privacy Policy Template

**Effective draft:** 14 August 2026  
**Status:** Developer-prepared template; legal review is required before store or commercial publication.

PuzzleForge is designed as a local-first game. This source version does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, cloud sync, multiplayer, crash-reporting, or payment SDKs. Settings, saved puzzles, progress, favorites, achievements, and local statistics are stored on the device.

The app can open external links or an email client only after a player chooses an action. The destination service then applies its own privacy policy. The app does not record whether a Buy Me a Coffee link was clicked.

Android or the operating system may include app data in device backups according to the user's platform settings and the app's backup rules. Exported backups are created only on explicit request and should not contain secrets.

Deleting app data from Settings removes PuzzleForge's locally stored records from the app container. Device backups or user-exported files may remain until deleted through their respective systems.

Questions: `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Material changes must be recorded in the changelog and presented where legally required.

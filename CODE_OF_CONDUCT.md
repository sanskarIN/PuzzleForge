# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in PuzzleForge welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, patient, and attentive to accessibility.
- Give and accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and community boundaries.
- Focus discussion on what is best for players and contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect reporter privacy where feasible, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants. Decisions may be appealed once by email with relevant context.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1 and should be interpreted in the spirit of building a safe, inclusive project.
